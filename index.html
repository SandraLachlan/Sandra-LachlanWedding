Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about celebrating with us in Kerala. All details can be edited as schedules are confirmed.

Our celebrations span three days in mid-February 2027:

  • Wednesday, 17 February 2027: Haldi Ceremony (morning) & Sangeet Celebration (night) at Tamarind Heritage.
  • Saturday, 20 February 2027: Traditional Hindu Wedding at Green Nest Kumbalangi.
  • Sunday, 21 February 2027: Wedding Reception at Mangalassery Pavilion.

Specific ceremony hours and muhurtham timings will be published as they are finalized.

We welcome you to embrace both traditional Indian elegance and classic formal wear:

  • Haldi Ceremony: Please refer to the Haldi color palette on the event card (joyful sunshine yellows, turmerics, marigold oranges, and festive coral pinks).
  • Sangeet Celebration: Any sort of colors of your choice! Feel free to wear any vibrant, joyful colors and festive kurtas or lehengas.
  • Traditional Hindu Wedding: Pastel pink and pastel green colors just like a lotus flower, as well as traditional Kerala Kasavu (cream and gold border sarees or mundu).
  • Wedding Reception: Formal western suits, evening gowns, cocktail dresses, or glamorous Indian silks and fusion wear.

February is one of the most delightful times of the year to visit Kerala! Days are typically sunny and warm with daytime temperatures around 28°C to 31°C (82°F – 88°F), and evenings cool down pleasantly with gentle coastal backwater breezes around 22°C to 24°C (72°F – 75°F). Lightweight breathable fabrics (cotton, linen, silk) are highly recommended.
